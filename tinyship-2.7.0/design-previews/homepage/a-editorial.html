A few things
to know.

What kind of photos should I use?

Use a clear, well-lit photo showing one person's face in each upload. A front-facing portrait with an unobstructed face works best.

Can I choose who sings first?

Yes. Person 1 starts the duet. Use the swap button between the uploads to switch the people and their selected voices.

What's included in the free preview?

The free preview is 3 seconds long. A credit costs $8.90 and generates one full 12-second rap video.

Do I need to write the lyrics?

No. Add an optional topic, occasion, or inside joke to give your duet a direction, and the lyrics are generated for you.
