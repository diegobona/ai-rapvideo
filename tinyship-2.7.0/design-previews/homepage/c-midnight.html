Ask us anything
What photos should I upload?

Use one clear, well-lit photo of each person with the face visible. Front-facing portraits work best. Choose photos you have permission to use.

Who raps first?

Person 1 starts the performance. Use the swap arrows between the photos to switch the people and their selected voices.

Can I choose what they rap about?

Yes. Add a topic, an inside joke, a birthday message, or a friendly roast in the optional topic field. Leave it blank if you want a surprise.

What’s included in the free preview?

You can try a 3-second video preview without payment. The full 12-second video costs $8.90 and uses 1 credit.

Do I need to record my own voice?

No recording is needed. Choose a voice for each person: young male, middle-aged male, older male, young female, or middle-aged female.
