Original AI-generated rap voice references, version rap-v2

Created for AI Rap Video using AutoDL's MiniMax H3 text-to-video workflow
(minimax_h3_z0901). The source performances use four fictional solo MCs,
two male voices and two female voices, with original English rap bars.
No uploaded user portrait, human reference recording or named artist is used
to create these stock voices.

The production prompts request isolated acapella rap: clipped consonants,
syncopated rhythmic speech and a 96 BPM pulse, without instruments or vocals
from a second performer. Reference words are not copied into user videos.

The source soundtracks are extracted, normalized, capped at 10 seconds and
encoded as 24 kHz mono MP3. These assets supply both voice identity and rap
delivery; they are not the previous Piper reading samples.

The generated delivery has not been independently verified by listening in
this environment. Actual performance and final video results are subject
to the user's manual listening review.
