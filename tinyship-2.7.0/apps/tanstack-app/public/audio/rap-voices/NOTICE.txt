Voice reference samples

Source: Piper English (US) Arctic medium synthesized speech samples
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/arctic/medium
Piper contributors, including Michael Hansen (Rhasspy).

male-one: CMU Arctic bdl, Piper speaker 7
male-two: CMU Arctic rms, Piper speaker 1
female-one: CMU Arctic slt, Piper speaker 2
female-two: CMU Arctic clb, Piper speaker 4

Modified for this application: cropped to at most 8 seconds, normalized,
converted to 24 kHz mono MP3. These are stock synthetic voice references;
they do not represent or recover the uploaded person's original voice.

Original dataset: Carnegie Mellon University CMU ARCTIC
